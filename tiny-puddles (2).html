Tiny Puddles

A tiny underwater farming sim.

You're a shrimp in a puddle. Collect seeds, carry them back to your little base, grow them, and sell the harvest to your neighbors for hats.

About the Game

Collect Seeds

Swim around the puddle and pick up all the different kinds of seeds.

Bring Them Home

Carry your haul back to your little base.

Plant & Grow

Plant them, give them time, and gather the fruit of your labor.

Sell for Hats

Trade your harvest to the neighbors. They pay in hats.
